About Me

I am a researcher with a PhD in Physical Chemistry and experience in scientific research, data analysis, computational modelling and scientific programming.

My research background includes physicochemical studies, thermodynamics, solution chemistry and industrial phosphoric acid processes. My doctoral research focused on understanding the physicochemical mechanisms associated with fouling during industrial wet-process phosphoric acid production.

I am currently expanding my research into scientific computing and cloud microphysics, using Python-based numerical modelling to investigate aerosol activation, supersaturation, droplet growth and warm-rain processes.

My interests lie at the intersection of physical chemistry, computational science and data-driven research, with a particular interest in applying numerical and computational methods to environmental and complex scientific problems.

Python Cloud Microphysics Modelling

Development and analysis of Python-based cloud microphysics simulations, focusing on aerosol activation, supersaturation, droplet growth and warm-rain processes.

The work includes sensitivity experiments, scientific data analysis and comparison with the KiD-A microphysics testbed.
